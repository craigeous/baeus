# research/

Research notes that feed planning. Each note gathers and distills cited context
(web, GitHub, local code, docs) on one topic so ADRs and specs are written
against evidence, not memory.

- **Authored by:** the researcher role (via `/loom:run` or `/loom:research`).
- **Reviewed by:** the plan evaluator (blind) when a note is submitted for
  Research Review.
- **Lifecycle:** Draft → Research Review → Approved. Approved notes are cited
  by ADRs and specs; they are descriptive records of what was found and are not
  updated in place once approved — new findings get a new note.

Notes are named `NNNN-short-slug.md` (or dated) and end with a citations list.
